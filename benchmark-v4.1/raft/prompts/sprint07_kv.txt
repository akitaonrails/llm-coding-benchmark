SPRINT 7 — Linearizable key/value store on your Raft.

Implement the `kvraft` package per CONTRACT.md: `StartKVServer`, a `Clerk` (MakeClerk/Get/Put/Append) talking
to the cluster. Requirements:
- LINEARIZABLE semantics: every client operation appears to take effect atomically at some point between its
  invocation and response; reads observe all prior acknowledged writes (route reads through the log or use a
  read-index/lease — do not serve stale reads from a follower).
- at-most-once: dedup retried client requests via client-id + sequence number (a retried Append must not be
  applied twice).
- snapshot the KV state when the raft log exceeds maxraftstate, and restore from snapshot on restart.
Handle leader changes and retries transparently in the Clerk. Commit at the end.
